# Privacy Policy — Last Tower: Merge & Survive **Last updated: November 19, 2026** Last Tower: Merge & Survive ("the Game") is a mobile game developed and published by an independent developer ("we," "us," or "our"). This Privacy Policy describes how information is stored, collected, used, and shared when you use the Game. ## 1. Information Stored on Your Device The Game stores information locally on your device to maintain your progress and preferences, including: - Your chosen in-game display name. - Virtual currencies, including coins and gems. - Game progression, achievements, unlocked items, and cosmetics. - Cards, relics, upgrades, and other game rewards. - Gameplay statistics, saved runs, and battle information. - Market, Battle Pass, and progression information. - Audio, vibration, and visual preferences. - Locally created Sound Studio settings and drafts. This information is used to provide gameplay functionality and preserve your progress. The Game does not currently require account registration or login. Its own gameplay systems do not transmit this saved information to a developer-operated game server. ## 2. Advertising and Third-Party Services The Game integrates Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC, to display optional rewarded advertisements. When you use the Game, Google's advertising technology may automatically collect and process information including: - IP address, which may be used to estimate general location. - Advertising identifiers and other device identifiers. - App interactions, including app launches, taps, and advertisement views. - Diagnostic and performance information, such as app launch times, hangs, and energy usage. Google may use this information for advertising delivery and measurement, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance. During development and testing, the Game may display Google test advertisements. Production advertisements may be enabled in future releases. For more information about Google's data practices, please visit: - Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy - Google Advertising Technologies: https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads ## 3. Advertising Choices and Consent Where required by applicable law, the Game will provide appropriate advertising consent requests or privacy choices. Users may also manage certain advertising preferences through their Android device settings. The availability of advertising consent and privacy options depends on the Game version, geographic region, and applicable legal requirements. ## 4. Information Sharing The Game's own gameplay systems do not currently upload locally stored game progress, player display names, or preferences to developer-operated servers. Google may collect and share certain information through its advertising SDK as described in this policy. We do not operate a separate player-account database or sell locally stored gameplay information. ## 5. Data Security The Game stores gameplay information locally on your device. Google states that information transmitted through its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. We take reasonable steps to maintain the security of the Game, but no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure. ## 6. Data Retention and Deletion Locally saved gameplay information generally remains on your device until removed through available game or device controls. You can remove local game information by clearing the Game's app data or uninstalling it, subject to your device's operating-system behavior. Information processed by Google is subject to Google's applicable data retention and deletion policies.
